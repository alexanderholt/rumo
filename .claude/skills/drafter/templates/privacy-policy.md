# Privacy Policy Framework

## Standard sections

### 1. Introduction
- Company name and contact information
- What this policy covers
- Effective date
- `[TAILOR]` Describe the specific product/service and what data it handles

### 2. Information we collect
- **Information you provide directly:** account registration, forms, communications
- **Information collected automatically:** device info, IP address, usage data, cookies
- **Information from third parties:** if applicable
- `[TAILOR]` List the specific categories of data THIS company collects
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` If the company handles PHI, financial data, or children's data — this section has heightened requirements

### 3. How we use your information
- Providing and improving the service
- Communications
- Legal compliance
- `[TAILOR]` Map to the company's actual data uses
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` Any use for marketing, profiling, or sharing with third parties needs careful language

### 4. How we share your information
- Service providers and vendors
- Legal requirements
- Business transfers
- With consent
- `[TAILOR]` List actual third parties and categories of sharing
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` If data is shared with third parties for their own purposes, this requires specific disclosure

### 5. Data retention
- How long data is kept
- Criteria for determining retention periods
- `[TAILOR]` Match to company's actual retention practices
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` Industry-specific retention requirements may apply (e.g., healthcare, financial services)

### 6. Your rights and choices
- Access, correction, deletion rights
- Opt-out mechanisms
- `[TAILOR]` Must reflect applicable privacy laws (CCPA rights differ from GDPR rights)
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` State-specific rights vary significantly — must match operating states

### 7. Security
- General description of security measures
- `[TAILOR]` Describe actual security practices without over-promising
- Do NOT list specific security technologies (creates liability if they change)

### 8. Children's privacy
- COPPA compliance statement if applicable
- Age restrictions
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` If the product could be used by minors, this section needs specific attention

### 9. International transfers
- If data crosses borders
- `[TAILOR]` Only include if the company operates internationally
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` GDPR transfer mechanisms if EU users are served

### 10. Changes to this policy
- How users will be notified of changes
- Effective date mechanism

### 11. Contact information
- How to reach the company with privacy questions
- `[TAILOR]` Specific contact details

## Regulatory overlays

Depending on the company's regulatory map, additional sections or modifications may be required:
- **HIPAA:** Business associate obligations, PHI handling, breach notification
- **CCPA/CPRA:** Right to know, right to delete, right to opt-out, do-not-sell
- **GDPR:** Legal basis for processing, DPO contact, supervisory authority, data subject rights
- **FERPA:** Education records handling
- **COPPA:** Verifiable parental consent, data minimization for children
- **State-specific:** Various state privacy laws with different requirements

The Document Drafter must check the regulatory map before drafting and apply the relevant overlays.
