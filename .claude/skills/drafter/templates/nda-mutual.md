# Mutual NDA Framework

## Standard sections

### 1. Parties
- `[TAILOR]` Both party names and addresses

### 2. Purpose
- `[TAILOR]` Why the parties are exchanging confidential information
- Keep broad enough to cover the relationship but narrow enough to be meaningful

### 3. Definition of confidential information
- What's included (business, technical, financial, operational information)
- What's excluded:
  - Publicly available information
  - Information already known to the receiving party
  - Information independently developed
  - Information disclosed by a third party without restriction
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` Overly broad definitions create enforcement problems; overly narrow ones create gaps

### 4. Obligations of receiving party
- Use only for the stated purpose
- Protect with reasonable measures (at least the same care as own confidential information)
- Limit access to those who need to know
- No reverse engineering

### 5. Term and duration
- How long the agreement lasts
- How long confidentiality obligations survive after termination
- `[TAILOR]` Standard is 2-3 years for the agreement, with obligations surviving 2-5 years. Industry-specific: trade secrets may warrant longer or indefinite periods.

### 6. Return or destruction of materials
- Obligation to return or destroy confidential information upon request or termination
- Exceptions for legally required retention

### 7. Permitted disclosures
- Court orders or legal process
- Regulatory requirements
- Notice requirement before compelled disclosure

### 8. No license or obligation
- NDA doesn't create a partnership or obligation to transact
- No license to IP is granted

### 9. Remedies
- Acknowledgment that breach may cause irreparable harm
- Right to seek injunctive relief
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` Specific performance and injunctive relief clauses vary by jurisdiction

### 10. General provisions
- Governing law and jurisdiction — `[TAILOR]` to company's state
- Entire agreement
- Amendment (written only)
- Severability
- Assignment
