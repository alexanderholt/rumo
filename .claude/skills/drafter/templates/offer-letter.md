# Employment Offer Letter Framework

## Standard sections

### 1. Position and start date
- `[TAILOR]` Job title, reporting structure, start date
- Full-time or part-time
- Exempt or non-exempt status
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` Exempt/non-exempt classification has specific legal criteria under FLSA and state law

### 2. Compensation
- `[TAILOR]` Base salary or hourly rate
- Payment frequency
- Bonus structure if applicable (make clear if discretionary)

### 3. Equity (if applicable)
- `[TAILOR]` Option grant, vesting schedule, exercise price
- Subject to board approval and plan terms
- Brief description only — full terms in separate option agreement
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` Equity promises in offer letters can create binding obligations. Keep high-level.

### 4. Benefits
- `[TAILOR]` Health insurance, PTO, other benefits
- Subject to plan terms and eligibility
- Company reserves right to modify benefits

### 5. At-will employment
- Employment is at-will — either party can terminate at any time
- This letter does not create a contract for a specific term
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` Some states or circumstances can create implied contracts. Language must be clear.

### 6. Contingencies
- Background check if applicable
- Proof of work authorization (I-9)
- Signing of CIIA
- `[TAILOR]` Any role-specific requirements (licensure, certifications)

### 7. Confidentiality and invention assignment
- Reference to CIIA (separate document)
- Must be signed as condition of employment

### 8. Acceptance
- Signature lines
- Expiration date for the offer
- `[TAILOR]` Reasonable deadline (typically 1-2 weeks)

## Regulatory overlays

- **State-specific:** Some states require specific disclosures in offer letters (e.g., pay transparency laws in CA, CO, NY, WA)
- **Equity:** If offering equity, 83(b) election information should be provided separately
