# Consulting Agreement Framework

## Standard sections

### 1. Engagement
- `[TAILOR]` Parties, effective date, scope of engagement
- Advisory vs. project-based distinction

### 2. Services and deliverables
- `[TAILOR]` Specific scope of consulting services
- Reporting and communication expectations
- Time commitment expectations

### 3. Compensation
- `[TAILOR]` Fee structure (hourly, project-based, retainer)
- Payment terms and invoicing
- Expense reimbursement
- Equity compensation if applicable (advisory shares)
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` If equity is involved, needs separate equity documentation

### 4. Term and termination
- Initial term
- Renewal mechanism
- Termination for convenience (with notice)
- Termination for cause

### 5. Confidentiality
- Mutual or one-way confidentiality obligations
- Definition and exclusions
- Survival period

### 6. Intellectual property
- IP assignment for work product
- License to pre-existing IP if incorporated into deliverables
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` Consultants typically retain more IP rights than employees. Be explicit about what's assigned vs. licensed.

### 7. Representations and warranties
- Authority to enter agreement
- No conflicts
- Professional standard of care

### 8. Independent contractor status
- Consultant is not an employee
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` Same classification risks as contractor agreement — see regulatory map for state-specific rules

### 9. Non-solicitation (if applicable)
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` Enforceability varies by state and context

### 10. Limitation of liability
- Cap on liability (typically fee amount)
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` Enforceability and reasonableness

### 11. General provisions
- Governing law — `[TAILOR]`
- Entire agreement
- Amendment
- Severability
