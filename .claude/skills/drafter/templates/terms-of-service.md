# Terms of Service Framework

## Standard sections

### 1. Acceptance of terms
- How users accept (click-through, continued use)
- Age requirements
- Authority to accept on behalf of an organization

### 2. Description of service
- `[TAILOR]` What the product/service actually does
- What's included vs. excluded
- Service availability and uptime (avoid specific SLA commitments unless intended)

### 3. User accounts
- Registration requirements
- Account security responsibilities
- Account termination

### 4. Acceptable use
- Prohibited activities
- `[TAILOR]` Industry-specific prohibitions (e.g., healthcare platforms may need to prohibit certain types of medical claims)
- Content standards if user-generated content exists

### 5. Intellectual property
- Company's IP ownership
- User's IP in any content they submit
- License grants (both directions)
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` IP provisions need careful drafting to avoid over-reaching or under-protecting

### 6. Payment terms (if applicable)
- Pricing and billing
- Cancellation and refunds
- Price changes
- `[TAILOR]` Match to actual pricing model

### 7. Disclaimers and limitations of liability
- Warranty disclaimers (AS-IS)
- Limitation of liability
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` These clauses are heavily regulated and vary by jurisdiction. Must be conspicuous.
- `[TAILOR]` Industry-specific disclaimers (e.g., "not medical advice," "not financial advice")

### 8. Indemnification
- User indemnification of company
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` Enforceability varies by jurisdiction and context

### 9. Dispute resolution
- Governing law
- Jurisdiction
- Arbitration clause (if desired)
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` Arbitration clauses have specific requirements and limitations
- `[TAILOR]` Match to company's state of incorporation and target users

### 10. Termination
- Company's right to terminate
- User's right to terminate
- Effect of termination on data and access

### 11. Modifications to terms
- How changes are communicated
- When changes become effective

### 12. General provisions
- Severability
- Entire agreement
- Waiver
- Assignment

## Regulatory overlays

Check the regulatory map for industry-specific requirements:
- **Healthcare:** Additional disclaimers about medical advice, HIPAA references
- **Financial services:** Required disclosures, regulatory compliance language
- **Education:** FERPA considerations, student data protections
- **Marketplace/platform:** Additional provisions for multi-sided platforms
