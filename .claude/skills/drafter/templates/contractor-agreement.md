# Independent Contractor Agreement Framework

## Standard sections

### 1. Parties and engagement
- `[TAILOR]` Company and contractor identification
- Effective date and term

### 2. Services
- `[TAILOR]` Description of services to be performed
- Deliverables and milestones if applicable
- Standard of performance

### 3. Compensation
- `[TAILOR]` Rate, payment schedule, invoicing requirements
- Expense reimbursement policy
- No benefits entitlement

### 4. Independent contractor status
- Contractor is not an employee
- Contractor controls manner and means of performing work
- No withholding, no benefits, no workers' comp
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` This is the highest-risk section. Classification depends on state law (ABC test in CA, economic reality test federally, common law test in other states). The Regulatory Analyst should confirm state-specific rules.
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` If the contractor works full-time, uses company equipment, or has a company email, the classification is at risk regardless of what the agreement says.

### 5. Confidentiality
- Obligation to protect company's confidential information
- Survives termination

### 6. Intellectual property
- Work product is work-for-hire (where applicable under copyright law)
- Assignment of IP rights where work-for-hire doesn't apply
- Pre-existing IP exclusions
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` Work-for-hire has a specific legal definition and doesn't apply to all types of work. Need assignment clause as backup.

### 7. Non-solicitation (if applicable)
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` Enforceability varies significantly by state. Some states (CA) generally prohibit.
- `[TAILOR]` Consider whether this is necessary and enforceable in the contractor's state

### 8. Termination
- Termination for convenience (either party, with notice period)
- Termination for cause
- Effect on compensation and IP

### 9. Representations and warranties
- Contractor has right to enter agreement
- Services will be performed professionally
- No conflicts with other obligations

### 10. Indemnification
- Contractor indemnifies company for claims arising from contractor's work
- `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` Scope and enforceability vary

### 11. General provisions
- Governing law — `[TAILOR]` to company's state
- Entire agreement
- Amendment
- Severability
- Assignment

## Regulatory overlays

- **California:** ABC test for classification. Presumption of employment unless all three prongs met.
- **State-specific:** Many states have their own classification tests. Check regulatory map.
- **Industry-specific:** Some industries have special classification rules (e.g., licensed professionals in healthcare).
